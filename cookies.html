Cookie Policy

Transparency regarding how we use cookies and personal browsing data.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They help us make the site function effectively, understand user preferences, and improve your overall experience.


2. How We Use Cookies

Taste Haven Restaurant uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: Required to keep our site secure, manage table reservations, and maintain essential site operations.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Help us measure visitor traffic, popular dishes, and overall website performance.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings, preferred currency, and general browsing preferences.

3. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can modify your browser settings to decline or clear cookies at any time. However, disabling certain functional cookies may impact your experience when browsing or making online reservations on our site.


4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our Cookie Policy, please get in touch via email at hijabsaghir2025@gmail.com or visit our Contact Page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers to help make your dining experience seamless.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday to Friday from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM, and Saturday to Sunday from 9:00 AM to 12:00 AM.

Do I need to make a reservation in advance?

While walk-ins are always welcome, we highly recommend booking a table in advance, especially during weekends and peak dinner hours.

Do you offer vegetarian and vegan options?

Yes, our menu features a wide variety of vegetarian and vegan items clearly marked across all categories including pizzas, pasta, and salads.

Is home delivery or takeaway available?

Yes, you can order takeaway directly through our contact line or place delivery orders via our food delivery partners.

Can you accommodate large parties or private events?

Absolutely! We offer group dining options and private event bookings. Please reach out through our contact page to plan your event.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash, major Credit/Debit Cards, and popular mobile wallet payment methods.

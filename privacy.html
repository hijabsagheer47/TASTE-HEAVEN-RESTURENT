Privacy Policy

We value your privacy and explain how information is collected, used, protected, and managed on our website.

Introduction

Welcome to Taste Haven Restaurant. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide while using our website and services.


Information We Collect

We may collect basic personal information when you use our website or contact us. This may include your name, email address, phone number, reservation details, and other information that you voluntarily provide.


How We Collect Information

Information may be collected when you submit a table reservation, contact form, newsletter subscription, catering request, or other online form available on our website.


How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to process reservations, respond to customer inquiries, manage catering requests, provide requested services, communicate important updates, and improve our website and restaurant services.


Reservation Information

Information submitted through our reservation form is used to manage your booking and communicate with you regarding your reservation.


Newsletter & Marketing

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we may use your email address to send restaurant updates, special offers, events, new menu items, and other relevant information.

You may unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time.


Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand visitor preferences, and provide a better browsing experience.

You can manage or disable cookies through your web browser settings.


Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services such as Google Fonts, Font Awesome, maps, analytics tools, social media services, or other external services.

These third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms of use.


Data Protection

We take reasonable measures to protect the information submitted through our website against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.


Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, manage reservations, respond to inquiries, and meet applicable business requirements.


Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third-party marketers. Information may only be shared with service providers when necessary to provide a requested service or operate our website.


Your Privacy Rights

You may contact us if you have questions about the personal information we hold about you. Where applicable, you may also request correction or removal of your personal information.


Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental or guardian involvement.


Website Security

Although we take reasonable steps to protect information, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information through online forms.


External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. Taste Haven Restaurant is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.


Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, website functionality, or privacy practices.

Any updates will be published on this page.


Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us through our Contact Us page.
